# MeetFlow AI

Transforms a meeting transcript into a structured analysis for human review.

## Language

**Transcript**: user-provided meeting text, treated as untrusted data rather than instructions.

**Meeting analysis**: a summary and lists of decisions, action items, risks, open questions and next steps extracted from a transcript.

**Action item**: a task mentioned in the meeting; owner, priority and deadline are recorded only when identified.

**Decision**: an explicit choice made in the meeting, distinct from a proposal or open question.

**Sample analysis**: a prewritten fictional result that demonstrates the interface without calling AI.
